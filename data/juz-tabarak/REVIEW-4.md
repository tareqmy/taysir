# Review notes: the 29th juz, unit 4 (surahs 68 and 67)

Keys are surah:ayah:word; “card” means a vocabulary card in 4.ts. This unit has many words that occur only once or twice in the Quran, so several renderings below are the common ones and not certain. The conventions of the Juz Amma notes apply here too: perfect-tense verbs after “when” or “if” are glossed in the present (for example 68:15:5, 67:7:4, 67:28:4, 67:30:4), and “the One who” is capitalised when it names God (67:1:2) but not for a person (67:20:3).

## Surah 68: Al-Qalam

- `68:6:2` “the afflicted” (also “the mad”, “the one put to trial”) and card `maftun`; the bi- of 68:6:1 is kept as a literal “in which of you”.
- `68:9:3` “you compromise” (also “soften”, “be lenient”, “flatter”) and card `yudhin`.
- `68:11:3` “with tale-bearing” and card `namim`. Card `hammaz` “slanderer, defamer” has the same root as the word in Al-Humaza (104:1:3), glossed the same way. Card `mannaa` “one who withholds, a hinderer”.
- `68:13:1` “harsh” (also “coarse”, “greedy”) and `68:13:4` “of doubtful origin” (also “base-born”, “notorious for evil”).
- Card `sarama` “to harvest, to pick the crop”: three words of this garden story share the root (68:17:9, 68:20:2, 68:22:7).
- `68:19:3` “a visitation” (literally “one that goes around”) and card `taif`.
- `68:20:2` “like the harvested” (also read “like black night” or “like barren ground”).
- `68:25:3` “determination” (also “anger”, “prevention”, or a place name).
- `68:28:2` “the most moderate of them” (also “the best”, “the most just”, “the middle one”).
- Card `asaa` “perhaps, it may be that” is a near-synonym of the existing card laalla.
- `68:39:5` “reaching” (usually explained as “binding”).
- `68:40:4` “a guarantor” and card `zaim` (also “leader”).
- `68:42:4` “a shin”: kept literal; the verse is read in different ways.
- Card `istataa` is close to the existing card qadara.
- `68:51:5` and `68:51:6` “would surely make you slip … with their eyes” (the idiom is explained differently).

Less doubtful, but loose: 68:14:1 “that” (usually “because”); 68:17:5 “the owners of” (elsewhere the same word is “companions of”); 68:44:6 “We will draw them on gradually”; 68:45:4 “My scheme”, chosen to match the existing card kayd; 68:46:3 “a payment” and 68:3:3 “a reward” for the same word; 68:48:11 “choked with grief”; 68:49:8 “on the bare land”.

## Surah 67: Al-Mulk

- Card `qadeer` “all-powerful” is close to the existing card qadir.
- `67:3:18` “cracks” (also “flaws”, “rifts”).
- `67:4:8` “driven back” (also “dazzled”, “humbled”, “dull”).
- `67:5:6` “and We made them”: the pronoun may point to the lamps or to the sky.
- Card `masir` “destination, journey’s end” is close to the existing card maab.
- `67:7:6` “a roaring” (also “a harsh gasp”, “sobbing”).
- `67:11:3` “so away” (also “far removed”, “destruction”).
- `67:13:8` “of what is in” (literally “of the owner of”).
- `67:14:3` “whoever”: it can be read as the subject or the object of the verb. `67:14:6` “the Subtle” (also “the Kind”).
- `67:15:9` “its regions (lit. shoulders)”; card `nushur` “the raising of the dead, rising up”.
- `67:16:2` and `67:17:3` “whoever”, with “in the sky”: kept literal and neutral.
- `67:17:12` “My warning” and `67:18:8` “My disapproval” (the ending “my” is not written in the text); card `nakir`.
- `67:19:6` “spreading their wings” (literally “ranged in rows”).
- `67:24:4` “multiplied you” and card `dharaa` (also “created”, “spread”).
- Card `sua` “to be evil; to distress”, used for the passive at 67:27:4. `67:27:12` “for it” and `67:27:13` “you call”: the meaning of this verb is disputed.

Less doubtful, but loose: 67:4:10 “exhausted”; 67:5:7 “missiles”; 67:15:6 “docile”; 67:17:9 “a storm of stones”; 67:21:9 “they persist”; 67:30:6 “sunken”.
