# Review notes: the 29th juz, unit 1 (surahs 77 to 75)

Keys are surah:ayah:word; “card” means a vocabulary card of this unit. The conventions at the top of the Juz Amma review notes apply here too: past-form verbs after “when” or “whoever” (for example the passive verbs in Al-Mursalat 8 to 11 and in Al-Qiyama 7 to 9 and 26 to 30) are glossed in the present or future.

## Surah 77: Al-Mursalat

- `77:1:1`, `77:2:1`: the oath participles are kept literal (“by those sent forth”, “those that blow fiercely”). No referent is named, as in Juz Amma; the later participles (“spread”, “separate”, “cast”) are literal too.
- `77:1:2` “in succession”: other readings are “for good” and “like the mane of a horse”.
- `77:2:2`: glossed “a fierce blowing”, but the corpus gives it the same lemma as “husks, chaff” in Al-Fil, so the app links it to that card.
- `77:6:1`, `77:6:3` “an excuse”, “a warning” (some read “to excuse or to warn”).
- `77:11:3` “are given their appointed time” (also “are brought to their time”).
- `77:17:2` “We make follow them”: who follows whom is read in more than one way.
- `77:23:3` “the able ones” (or “those who measure”; the existing card is “able”).
- `77:25:4` “a container” (a place that gathers).
- `77:32:4` “the castle” (another reading is “logs”); `77:33:2` “camels” (another reading is “ropes”).
- `77:50:2` “report” (also “statement, message”).
- Cards `waqi` “occurring, about to happen”; `furijat` (close to “to split open” in Al-Inshiqaq); `qarar` and `mustaqarr` (close to each other and to “resting place, bed”); `nataqa` (close to “to speak”).

## Surah 76: Al-Insan

- `76:8:3`, `76:8:4` “on” and “love of it”: “despite their love for it” or “out of love for Him”; the “it” may be the food or Allah.
- `76:10:6`, `76:10:7` “frowning”, “harsh” (a grim and distressing day).
- `76:11:6` “and granted them” (literally “caused them to meet”).
- `76:16:4` “they have measured it out”: who measures, and whether it means “by their desire”, is debated.
- `76:21:1` “above them” (the reading of this word varies).
- `76:27:4` and `75:20:4` “the fleeting life” (literally “the hurried one”, taken as the present life).
- `76:28:4` “their build” (constitution, frame, joints).
- Cards `ittabaa` (close to “to follow”), `aajila`, `yadhar` (close to “to leave, to abandon”), `mathal` (close to “the like of”), `aadda` (the other “prepare” word, at 76:4:2, is a separate lemma and has no card).

## Surah 75: Al-Qiyama

- `75:1:1`: “no” before an oath; teachers read it as emphasis or as negation. `75:2:4` “the self-blaming”.
- `75:5:4` “so as to act wickedly”: the sense of the clause varies (“to go on sinning in the future”).
- `75:8:1` “is eclipsed” (the verb literally means to sink or lose light).
- `75:14:5` “insight” (also “a witness against himself”).
- `75:25:5` “a back-breaking calamity”; `75:27:3` “a charmer” (or “one who carries the soul up”); `75:30:4` “the driving”.
- `75:34:1`: “closer”, a comparative used as a warning, usually rendered “woe to you”; it is kept literal in all four places.
- Cards `khasafa`, `lawwama` “much-blaming”, `awla` “closer, more deserving”, `ajila` “to hurry” (close to “to hasten; to strive”).
