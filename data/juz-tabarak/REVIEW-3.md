# Review notes: the 29th juz, unit 3 (surahs 71 to 69)

Keys are `surah:ayah:word`; “card” means a vocabulary card in 3.ts. The points that apply to the whole unit come first. This is a large unit (three surahs, 124 verses), so the list is longer than for a short surah.

Points that apply to the whole unit:

- **Past-form verbs after “when”, “if” or “whoever”** are glossed in the present or future, as in Juz Amma: “touches him” (70:20:2, 70:21:2), “is blown” (69:13:2), “seeks” (70:31:2), “is given” (69:19:3, 69:25:3). After law (“if”, contrary to fact) the past is kept, as “had” and “would have”: `69:44:2` “he had made up”, “We would have seized” (69:45:1). A teacher should confirm this exception.
- **Names of peoples and people** (Nuh, Thamud, Aad, Pharaoh) are kept as names. The five names in verse 23 of Nuh are written as names only; the glosses do not say who or what they were.
- **Very rare cards.** Cards for words that occur only once or twice in the whole Quran (halu, jazu, manu, mamun, arja, nafkha, waa, qadiya, khafiyah, bisat) could be dropped if a lesson feels heavy; each is there because its verse is hard to follow without it.
- **The most technical notes** are the singular verb before its doers (lesson maarij-1, verse 4 of Al-Ma’arij), the numbers three to ten taking the opposite gender (lesson haqqa-2), the dual verb ending (lesson haqqa-3, verse 14 of Al-Haqqa) and the pause letter in the ending -iyah (lesson haqqa-4).

## Surah 71: Nuh

- `71:4:3` “of”: the min before “your sins” is read as partitive (“some of”) or as plain “of”.
- `71:4:8` “appointed” (literally “named”) and card `ajal` “term, fixed period”: close in sense to the existing card miqat (“appointed time”).
- `71:11:2` “the sky”: kept literal; many translations say “rain”.
- `71:13:4`, `71:13:6` “you hope”, “dignity”: the verse is read in several ways (hope for dignity from Allah; show Him reverence; ascribe majesty to Him). Kept literal.
- Card `ghaffar` “one who forgives again and again”: close to the existing card ghafur (“forgiving, oft-forgiving”).
- Cards `makara` “to plan cunningly” and `makr` “cunning plan, scheme”: near-synonyms of the existing cards yakid and kayd.
- `71:23:7`: Wadd, and the four names after it (Suwa’, Yaghuth, Ya’uq, Nasr), spelled as names. Check the transliterations.
- `71:26:10` “any dweller”: the word means roughly “anyone living in a house”.
- `71:27:10` and card `kaffar` “persistent disbeliever”: the intensive form; “ungrateful” is also common.
- `71:28:4` and card `walidayn` “and for my two parents” / “both parents”: a dual with “my” joined on; the existing card walid is the singular.

## Surah 70: Al-Ma’arij

- `70:1:3` “for a punishment”: “asked for” versus “asked about”. The next word, “that will occur”, is the participle “falling”.
- `70:8:4` and card `muhl` “like molten metal” / “molten metal; dregs of oil”: the meaning is disputed (molten brass, dregs of oil, melted metal).
- `70:10:3`, `70:10:4` “a close friend”: the same spelling as the existing card hamim (“scalding water”). The note in lesson maarij-2 says so; the same word returns at 69:35:5.
- `70:15:3` “a blazing flame”: the corpus tags the word as a proper name (Laza, a name or level of Hell). Kept as the literal meaning.
- `70:16:2` “the scalps”: the word is read as scalps, skin or limbs. The word before it, “stripping away”, is also loose.
- Cards `halu`, `jazu`, `manu` “anxious, restless”, “impatient, fretful”, “withholding, grudging”: each occurs only once; other renderings (greedy, hasty, miserly) are common.
- Card `mamun` “safe, secure from harm”: close to the existing card amin (“trustworthy, secure”).
- `70:30:2` “with”: the preposition ala after “except”; literally “upon”.
- `70:36:5` and card `muhti` “hastening”: other renderings add “with necks outstretched” or “staring”.
- `70:40:1` “so no”: the la before an oath (also at 69:38:1). Kept literal.
- `70:43:8` “something set up”: nusub is read as a goal, a banner or stone idols.

## Surah 69: Al-Haqqa

- `69:1:1` and card `haqqah` “the Inevitable Reality”: also “the Sure Reality” or “the Reality”.
- `69:5:4` “by the overpowering one”: the word is read as a cry, a blast, or the transgression itself.
- `69:6:5` and card `sarsar` “fierce”: the sense is “icy cold” or “roaring”.
- `69:7:7` “in succession”: husum is read as “consecutive” or “uprooting”.
- `69:7:15` and card `khawiya` “hollow”: also “fallen down”, “collapsed”.
- `69:9:5` “and the overturned cities”: “cities” is added; the word is a feminine plural participle.
- `69:14:4` “then both are crushed”: a passive verb with the dual ending (feminine); the subjects are “the earth” and “the mountains”.
- `69:17:1` “and the angels”: a singular collective, as at 89:22.
- `69:24:5` and card `aslafa` “you sent ahead”, “to do beforehand”: close to the existing card qaddama (“to send ahead, to put forward”).
- `69:27:3` and card `qadiya` “the decisive end”: understood as death.
- `69:36:5` “foul discharge”: ghislin is disputed (pus, washings of wounds). Compare 78:25:3.
- `69:41:6`, `69:42:5` “(emphasis)”: the corpus tags ma as a relative word here; the usual reading is “how little”.
