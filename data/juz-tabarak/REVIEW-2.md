# Review notes: the 29th juz, unit 2 (surahs 74 to 72)

Points that apply across the unit:

- Perfect-tense verbs after “when” or a condition word are glossed in the present, as in Juz Amma (“is sounded”, “they see”, “believes”, “submits”). A teacher should confirm the convention. Perfect verbs that state a fact (“He knows”) are glossed in the present too.
- After a past form of “to be”, a present verb is glossed with “-ing” (“we were … plunging”); after “did not”, the verb “to be” is glossed “we be” (74:43:3 and 74:44:2), as in Juz Amma (“did not … be”).
- Passive verbs are glossed passively (“is sounded”, “was filled”, “is handed down”).
- “Mankind” is used for the same word at 74:25:5, 74:29:2, 74:31:57 and 74:36:2; “determined” for the same verb at 74:18:3, 74:19:3 and 74:20:4, to match the existing card; “the unjust” at 72:14:5 and 72:15:2.

## Surah 74: Al-Muddaththir

- `74:1:2` “the one wrapped in a cloak”; `73:1:2` (Al-Muzzammil) “the one wrapped up”. Both are literal; the two forms differ slightly (cloak versus wrapping in garments).
- `74:4:1` “and your clothes”: clothes, self or heart (a contested reading). `74:5:1` “the impurity”: filth, sin or idols.
- `74:6:2`, `74:6:3` “give as a favour”, “expecting more”: several readings (do not give in order to get more; do not count your favour as great).
- `74:17:2` “a steep ascent” (and “I will burden him” at 74:17:1).
- `74:19:3` “he determined” (also “planned”, “plotted”).
- `74:24:6` “is handed down” (passed on, related from earlier people).
- `74:29:2` “for mankind” may mean “the skin” (and “scorching” at 74:29:1 also “blackening”).
- `74:30:2` “nine” (with “and ten”): two words for one number, “nineteen”; what is counted is not stated in the verse.
- `74:31:3` “the companions of” the Fire: usually understood as keepers or guardians; kept literal.
- `74:33:3` “it departs” (a variant reading is also recorded).
- `74:36:1` “a warning”: “a warner” is also possible, and who is meant is disputed.
- `74:47:3` “the certainty”: understood as death.
- `74:50:3` “startled”; `74:51:3` “a lion” (some say hunters or archers).
- `74:56:8` “the one worthy of” (repeated at 74:56:10, with “piety” at 74:56:9): also rendered “worthy of being feared” and “worthy to forgive”.

## Surah 73: Al-Muzzammil

- `73:6:2` “the rising of” (the night vigil, or the early part of the night); `73:6:6` “in impact” (the noun is also read as treading, or harmony of heart and tongue); `73:7:5` “a busy occupation” (literally swimming, gliding).
- `73:11:4` “the comforts”.
- `73:17:2` “will you guard yourselves”: whether the day in 73:17:5 is what they disbelieve or what they guard against is not settled by the grammar.
- `73:20:20` “He knows” for a perfect verb (also at 73:20:31); `73:20:24` “so He relented”.
- `73:20:59` “a loan” (a loan to Allah, with “and lend” at 73:20:57).

## Surah 72: Al-Jinn

- `72:3:3` “the majesty of”: also glory, grandeur or good fortune.
- `72:4:4` “our fool”: who is meant is disputed.
- `72:6:11` and card `rahaq` “burden” (also folly, arrogance, fear).
- `72:8:8` “and flames”: meteors or flames; no side is taken.
- `72:11:5` “below”: the phrase is rendered “less than that” or “not so”.
- `72:12:5` “we can frustrate” (escape, overcome).
- `72:14:5` “the unjust” (one who departs from justice).
- `72:17:10` “arduous” (also steep, ever-increasing).
- `72:18:2` “the mosques” (places of prostration; some read the parts of the body used in prostration).
- `72:19:10` “crowded together”: who is meant by “they” is disputed.
- `72:27:7` “He makes go” (the verb is the same as the card `salaka`); “watchers” at 72:27:13.

## Cards

- `bashar` “mortal, human” is close to the existing cards for “human being” and “people, mankind”.
- `salaka` “to lead into, to make enter” (a verb that also means to follow a way); `aarada` “to turn aside from” is close to the existing card for “to turn away”; `tahhara` “to cleanse” is close to the existing card for “to purify”.
- `dara` “to know, to perceive” is close to the existing cards for “to know” and “to let someone know”.
- `fitna` “trial, test” (also persecution, temptation); `daraba` “to strike; to travel; to set forth (a parable)” is broad.
- The word for “when” at 72:13:2 and 72:19:2 has the same spelling as the existing card for “except; not yet; when”, so the unit seeds no second card for it.
