# Teacher review: where to look first

Every gloss, vocabulary meaning and lesson note in `data/juz-amma/` and `src/lib/content/juz-amma/` is a **draft**. The authors of each unit listed the entries they were least sure of. Check these first. Keys are `surah:ayah:word`; “card” means a vocabulary card in `N.ts`.

Points that apply across all units:

- **Perfect-tense verbs after conditional or “when” words** (`idha`, `man`) are glossed in the present or future (“brings out”, “will know”), as common translations do, although the corpus tag is past. A teacher should confirm the convention.
- **Capitalisation of title-like terms** (the Hour, the Garden, the Crusher, the Striking Calamity, the Spirit, Hell) is not consistent between units.
- **Near-duplicate cards**: `aghnat` (unit 2) is probably the same verb as `aghna` (existing); `imru` and `mar` (unit 6) are near-synonyms; `uzlifat` and `muqarrab` (unit 5) are close.
- **Two cards share a spelling**: `ma` “what” and `manafiya` “not”; `idha` “when” and `idhasudden` “behold”. The exercises avoid offering one as a wrong answer for the other.
- The corpus tag disagrees with the usual reading at `80:10:3` (“you are distracted”) and `79:32:2` (“He anchored them”). The glosses follow the meaning.

## Unit 1: surahs 104 to 97

- `104:1:3`, `104:1:4`: “slanderer”, “backbiter” (other renderings: scorner, mocker, defamer).
- `104:7:2` “it rises”; `104:8:3` “sealed” (loosely “closed over”).
- `103:1:1` “by time”: time, era or afternoon.
- `97:1:5`, `97:2:5`, `97:3:2` and card `qadr`: “decree” versus “power”. `97:3:6` “months” for a singular word. `97:4:7` “from” for “min kulli amr”.
- `100:1:1` to `100:3:2`: the running, striking and raiding participles are literal; no referent is named.
- `100:9:4` “is overturned”; `100:10:1` “and is brought out” (least certain).
- `99:5:3` and card `awha`: “has inspired” versus “revealed” or “commanded”.
- `101:5:4` “the fluffed up”; `101:9:2` “an abyss”.
- `98:1:9` “breaking away”; card `hanif` “upright, true to the faith”; card `aata` “to grant; to bring”.
- Cards `zakat`, `mushrik`, `jahim`, `fuad`, `layla`, `shaheed` (the last two reworded to stay distinct from units 2 and 4).

## Unit 2: surahs 96 to 91

- `91:10:4` “he buried it” and `91:12:2` “rose up” (alternatives: “corrupted it”, “rushed forth”).
- `91:14:7` “and He levelled it”: referent unclear. `91:15:2` “fears”: subject ambiguous.
- `92:11:6` “he falls” (falls, perishes). `92:6:2` “the best” and card `husna`: debated.
- Card `mamnun` “cut off, interrupted” and `95:6:9`: “cut off” versus “reproached as a favour”.
- `95:8:3`, `95:8:4`: “the most just of” (or “wisest”), “the judges”. `95:7:2` “denies you”. `95:4:6` “form” (stature, mould).
- `96:2:4` “a clinging clot”; `96:18:2` “the Zabaniyah” (kept as a name); `96:15:5` “We will surely drag”; `96:17:2` “his gathering”.
- `93:2:3` “it grows still”; `93:3:2` “has forsaken you”; `93:10:4` “rebuke”; `93:11:4` “so proclaim”.
- `94:7:3` “then strive” (strive versus stand for worship); `94:3:2` “weighed down”.
- Cards `tur`, `duha`, `istaghna` (a paraphrase of form X), ids `ansub` (the particle “that”) and `atoa` are awkward.

## Unit 3: surahs 90 to 88

- `90:1:1` “no”: before an oath, emphatic or negating. `90:2:2` and card `hill`: “free of restriction” (resident, free, lawful).
- `90:6:2` and card `ahlaka`: “squandered” versus “destroyed”. `90:13:2` and card `raqaba`: “a slave (lit. neck)”.
- `90:4:5` “hardship”; `90:11:2` “he has attempted”; `90:14:6` “famine”; `90:15:3` “kinship”; `90:16:4` “destitution” (literally dust).
- `89:5:6` and card `hijr` “intellect; restraint”; `89:13:4` “a whip of”; `89:14:3` “surely on the watch”; `89:21:3`, `89:21:5`, `89:21:6` “is crushed”, “a pounding”.
- `89:15:4`, `89:16:3` “ever”: the particle after “when”. `89:15:8` “and favours him”. `89:22:3` “and the angels” for a singular collective.
- `88:3:1`, `88:3:2` “toiling”, “weary”; `88:5:4` “boiling”; `88:8:3` “blissful”; `88:22:3` “a controller”.
- Cards `hadith` “report, news” and `jariya` “flowing, running”.
- The notes on the accusative after “inna”, the dual ending, and the `maf‘ūla` shape are the most technical.

## Unit 4: surahs 87 to 84

- `84:6:8` “so you will meet it”: what the suffix refers to. `84:16:1` “so no”: the `la` before an oath.
- `84:2:3` and card `haqqa`: passive “is obliged”. `84:23:4` “they keep inside”. `84:17:3` “it gathers”. `84:25:9` “cut off” (or “uninterrupted”).
- `85:4:1` “were destroyed” (literally “killed”). `85:3:1`, `85:3:2` “a witness”, “one witnessed”: identity disputed. `85:1:3` and card `buruj`: “constellations; towers”.
- `85:8:2` and card `naqama`; `85:13:4` and card `yuid`; `85:12:2` and card `batsh`; `85:16:1` “Doer”.
- `86:7:5` “and the ribs”; `86:11:3`, `86:12:3` “the return”, “the splitting” (literal); `86:1:2`, `86:2:4` “the night-comer”; `86:8:3` “returning him”.
- `86:4:4` and card `lamma` “except; not yet; when”; `86:9:2` and card `balaa`.
- `87:2:3` “and proportioned”; `87:5:2` “dry debris”; `87:7:7` and card `jahr`; `87:16:4` and card `dunya`.
- Cards `fasl` “decisive; separation” and `quran` “Quran; recitation” are broad.

## Unit 5: surahs 83 to 81

- `81:17:3` “it departs” (or “closes in”); `81:2:3` “are dimmed”; `81:6:3` “are set ablaze” (or “overflow”); `81:4:2` “the pregnant she-camels”; `81:4:3` “are neglected”.
- `81:15:3`, `81:16:1`, `81:16:2`: “the retreating ones”, “the running ones”, “the hiding ones” (no “stars” interpretation added). Card `jawar`.
- `81:20:6` and card `makin`; `81:24:5` “withholding” (or “miserly”); `81:22:3` and card `majnun`; `81:8:2` “the girl buried alive”.
- `83:15:6` “surely veiled”; `83:31:6` “jesting”; `83:25:3` “pure wine”; `83:1:2` “the defrauders”; `83:2:4` “from (lit. on)”; `83:14:3` “has covered”.
- `82:8:2`, `82:8:4` “whatever”, “(emphasis)”; `82:7:3`, `82:7:4` and card `adala`.
- Passive-lemma cards `fujjirat`, `kuwwirat`, `suyyirat`, `zuwwijat`, `nushirat`, `uzlifat` are glossed “to be …”. Cards `mabuth`, `kawkab`.

## Unit 6: surahs 80 and 79

- `79:1:2` “violently” (plunging or drowning); `79:2:2` “with ease”; `79:3:1` to `79:5:1` and `79:4:2` are deliberately non-interpretive.
- `80:15:2` “scribes” (or “emissaries”); `80:19:4` “and determined him”; `80:17:3` “how” (exclamatory); `80:17:4` “disbelieving is he”.
- `80:28:2` “and herbage”; `80:31:2` “and grass”; `80:37:6` “that suffices him”; `80:41:2` “gloom”.
- `79:10:5` “the former state”; `79:14:3` “on the surface”; `79:16:6` “Tuwa” (taken as a valley’s name); `79:34:3` “the overwhelming calamity”; `79:13:3` “a shout”.
- `79:42:5` and card `mursa` “anchorage; time of arrival”; card `hawa` “desire, whim”; card `bunayy` “sons”; cards `idhasudden`, `idhan`, `imru`, `mar`.

## Unit 7: surah 78

- `78:25:3` “and a foul fluid” (disputed); `78:25:2` and card `hamim` “scalding water”.
- `78:33:1` “and young maidens”; `78:38:3` “the Spirit”; `78:14:3` “the rain clouds”; `78:12:4` “strong”; `78:15:1` “so that We bring forth”.
- `78:28:3` “with denial” and `78:35:6` “falsehood” for the same lemma; `78:36:5` “sufficient” versus `78:27:5` “a reckoning”.
- `78:29:4` “in writing”; `78:38:9` “the one whom”; `78:39:5`, `78:39:6` perfect verbs glossed in the present; `78:40:7` “the person”; `78:23:3` “for ages”.
- Cards `taghi`, `muttaqi`, `ittakhadha`, `raja`, `zawj`, `mihad`.
